Privacy notice

How we look after the details you send us.

Draft for review. This is a starting point. Please have it checked before launch so it matches how the office actually handles personal data.

Who we are

Cllr Noel Connolly, 6 Claregate St, Kildare Town, Co. Kildare R51 Y243. Phone 087 361 1407, email mail@noelconnolly.ie.

What we collect and why

When you use the contact form we receive your name, area, email address, phone number, how you'd like to be contacted, the topic you chose and what you wrote. We use these only to deal with your query and to contact you about it, including making representations on your behalf to public bodies where you have asked us to.

The legal basis is your consent and, for representations to public bodies, section 40 of the Data Protection Act 2018, which allows elected representatives to process personal data when acting on a constituent's behalf.

Where it is kept

Messages are delivered by email to the office and recorded in the office's casework system. We do not sell or share your details for marketing, and we do not add you to any mailing list without asking.

How long we keep it

We keep casework records for as long as needed to deal with your query and any follow-up, and review them regularly.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing mail@noelconnolly.ie. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

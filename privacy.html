How we look after the information you send us.

Draft for review. Please check this matches how the office actually works before the site goes live. Last updated 14 October 2026.

Who we are

Cllr Noel Connolly, Kildare County Council, is responsible for the information you send through this website (the "data controller"). Office: 4 Market Square, Kildare, R51 X458, Ireland. Phone 087 361 1407, email mail@noelconnolly.ie.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, area, email address, phone number, how you'd like to be contacted, the topic you chose, what you searched for, and your message. We don't collect anything else about you through this website.

Why we use it, and the legal basis

We use your details only to deal with your query: to reply to you, keep a record of what we've done, and, where you ask us to, raise the matter with the council, a government department or another public body.

The legal basis is section 40 of the Data Protection Act 2018, which allows an elected representative to process your information, including sensitive information such as health details, when you ask for help. Please only include sensitive details if we need them to help you.

Campaign messages (for example offering to volunteer) are not casework. We use them only with your consent, keep them separately, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

We will not add you to a newsletter or mailing list unless you ask us to.

Who sees it

  • Noel and the people working in his office, who are bound by confidentiality.
  • The public body responsible for your issue, if you ask us to raise it with them.
  • Our IT providers, who act only on our instructions: Google (which delivers form messages by email) and the providers of the office's casework system, which is hosted in Ireland. Google may process data outside the EU, under the safeguards EU law requires.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website. Like any web host, it records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security.

How long we keep it

  • While we work on your query: for as long as it's open.
  • Resolved queries: 2 years after they're resolved, because people often come back about related issues. Then they are deleted.
  • Copies made while delivering your message (the notification email and a backup spreadsheet row): deleted after 30 days, once the query is in our casework system.
  • Campaign messages: until 6 months after the next election, or sooner if you ask.
  • Spam: not kept.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to stop using it, or withdraw your consent where we rely on it. Contact the office using the details above. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28, or at dataprotection.ie.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies or use analytics or tracking. Its fonts are stored on the site itself, and the map is a link to Google Maps rather than an embedded map, so nothing is loaded from other companies while you browse. If that changes, we'll ask for your permission first.
